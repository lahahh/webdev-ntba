Enhance `project/about.html` with `h1`, `h2`, and `h3` headings; at least three paragraphs; an unordered list of five or more activities; an ordered list of three or more joining steps; and at least two images with meaningful `alt` text. Add a `Photo Gallery` section with at least three images and captions.

Save with `git add project/about.html`, commit `HW3: Enhance About page with rich content`, and push.
